Light Academy: School Website and Student Records
A live website for a private school in Osogbo, Osun State, Nigeria. It presents the school to parents and keeps the school's student records and management details in one place.
Live site: https://lightacademysite.vercel.app Status: Live and in active use by the school
What it does
Tells parents what the school offers: programmes, teachers, gallery and news.
Lets parents send enquiries through a form that reaches the school directly.
Keeps student records and management details for the school.
Installs on a phone like an app and works well on mobile.
Tech stack
React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL, Progressive Web App (PWA) technologies, deployed on Vercel.
